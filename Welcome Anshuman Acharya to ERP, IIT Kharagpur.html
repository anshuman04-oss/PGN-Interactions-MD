ERP Complaint / Request Section

  • For technical issues pertaining to the ERP system, including login problems, system errors, slow performance, or any unexpected behavior, please submit a complaint through this portal. ERP Grievance Section

  • This is a centralized platform to raise grievances in a structured and transparent manner. Grievance authorities can monitor, review, and manage submitted cases, while assigned personnel can take necessary actions for resolution.

  • This is different from ERP Complaints and Internal Complaints of Apna IIT KGP
    • For general maintenance issues related to Civil, Water, Sanitary, Electrical, Internet etc. please use Apna IIT KGP.
